Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

QuaSi ("we," "us") develops quantum-enhanced AI solutions for computational fluid dynamics and simulation. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information: your name, email address, company, and industry when you reach out to us via our contact form or email.
  • Usage data: basic analytics about how you interact with our website, including pages visited and referral sources.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies necessary for website functionality.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide information about our solutions.
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it.
  • To send relevant updates about our technology (only with your consent).
  • To keep our service secure and prevent abuse.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us operate our website (hosting, analytics) under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain our business relationship. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies for website functionality. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at aditya@quasi.digital.

This document is provided for general information and is not legal advice. We recommend reviewing it with qualified counsel before relying on it.
